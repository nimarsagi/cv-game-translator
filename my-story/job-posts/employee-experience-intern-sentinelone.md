## What Are We Looking For?

We're looking for people who are relentlessly curious and committed to continuous learning. AI is reshaping every function across our business, and we enable every team member, regardless of role or level, to build fluency in AI tools and concepts. Those who thrive here actively seek out new solutions, experiment thoughtfully, and apply what they learn to drive better, faster, smarter outcomes.

As an Employee Experience Intern you will help create a meaningful and engaging workplace for our employees through a blend of administrative support, program coordination, and creative projects. You'll assist with onboarding logistics, benefits documentation, wellness initiatives, internal communications, and in-person events. The ideal candidate is organized, proactive, and passionate about building an inclusive and high-impact employee experience. This is a great opportunity to gain hands-on exposure to multiple areas of HR, culture, and operations at a fast-growing tech company.

## What Will You Do?

**Onboarding**

- Support logistics, materials, and checklists for onboarding
- Coordinate first-day experiences and gather feedback

**Benefits & Wellness**

- Organize and track benefit usage
- Maintain internal benefits documentation
- Research and suggest perk/wellness improvements

**Employee Experience & Engagement**

- Assist in planning in-person events and team activities
- Support internal comms, celebrations, and culture initiatives

**HR Operations & Admin**

- Maintain records and systems
- Help with documentation and internal process improvements

**Projects You Could Own or Support**

- Benefits overview guide
- In-person events calendar
- Employee experience touchpoint mapping
- Pulse surveys and feedback reports

## What Skills and Knowledge Will You Bring?

- Bachelor's or Master's student in Human Resources, Business Administration, Psychology, or related field
- Strong organizational and project management skills
- Creative and proactive in thinking about ways to enhance team culture
- Excellent communication skills and attention to detail
- A genuine interest in employee engagement, events, and wellness
